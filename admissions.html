Admissions

Interested in enrolling your child?

Bayt ul Uloom hasn't opened its doors yet — but when we do, our founding families will be the first to know. Register your interest today, and we'll reach out as our enrollment timeline takes shape.

Why Register Early

Three reasons to get on our list now.

Be first to know

Families on our interest list will be the first to hear when we set an opening date and open formal enrollment.

Shape our planning

Knowing how many families are interested — and in which grades — helps us plan staffing, space, and our launch timeline.

Join our founding families

Early families help shape the community and culture of Bayt ul Uloom from its very first year.
